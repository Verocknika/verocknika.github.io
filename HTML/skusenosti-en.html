My work journey

My experience is not just about work positions. It is about people, technology, solving problems and an eye for detail that has been developing since childhood.

Early foundation

Detailing and an eye for detail from a young age

My work ethic started to form when I was 12 years old in a family business. It was not special treatment, but hard work and real experience. My role was to prepare bicycles for sale - from deep cleaning to carefully touching up small marks. That was where I understood that a product must be in excellent condition, and that every result depends on discipline, responsibility and attention to detail, which is just as important in my work in IT today.

How my experience developed over time

  1. 2012 First technical repair
  2. 2015 - 2019 Technical independence
  3. 2019 - 2025 Working with people
  4. 2024 - 2025 Support and IT studies
  5. 2026 Software testing
2012

Laptop repair

My technical journey began with improvisation at the age of 17. When the display on my laptop cracked and a professional repair was not within budget, I picked up a screwdriver and successfully replaced it myself. Gradually, I was no longer afraid to go deeper - from cleaning the inside and repasting components to replacing the heatsink and fan, and eventually rebuilding the whole chassis. Without previous experience, relying only on determination and logic, I learnt that if something does not work, there is usually a way to fix it.

2015 - 2019

Car repair

A driving licence is a symbol of independence for me, and I protect that independence by not being afraid of technical challenges. When needed, I was not afraid to pick up tools and handle repairs myself, such as repairing a carburettor or replacing a windscreen wiper mechanism. Being able to deal with technical problems gives me confidence, but my real passion remains the freedom of driving, reflected in more than 150,000 kilometres driven across Europe.

2019 - 2025

Identifying needs

Years of direct contact with people gave me strong empathy and a natural understanding of different types of user needs. I can listen patiently, identify the core of a problem and then explain complex solutions clearly, whether in person or online. This careful approach has also brought me recognition and awards.

2024

Customer experience

In 2024, on the Staffino platform, I achieved 100% customer satisfaction, but for me it was never only about numbers. In today's often impersonal world, I enjoyed giving people the feeling that someone was truly listening to them. The greatest reward was seeing that people valued my help - from personal thanks to ice cream or sweets from customers I had helped. This customer-focused approach and ability to listen are also essential in software development and testing.

2024

Display repair

In 2024, when my mum accidentally dropped a brand-new phone and broke the display, I decided to try repairing it myself, even though I had no previous experience. With courage, patience and a video tutorial, I managed to take the device apart and replace the display successfully. This experience confirmed that I have a natural eye for detail and an ability to look for solutions where others might give up.

2024 - 2025

Helpdesk

Technical support for end users and corporate clients taught me quick diagnosis of hardware and software issues and incident prioritisation. I care about communication during outages being not only professional, but also clear and human.

2024 - Present

Tomas Bata University - Faculty of Applied Informatics

I am studying Software Engineering. I received a scholarship for excellent academic results. I am currently working on my bachelor's thesis topic, 'Gamified Cryptology', which connects data security with interactive learning.

2026

Testing

Manual testing of web applications with an emphasis on functionality and user experience. I am currently analysing technical solutions and portal structures in line with international quality standards, while preparing to obtain an ISTQB certification.
